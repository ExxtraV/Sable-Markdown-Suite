# Northwatch: Outline

_Sample text written by Claude Code._

Chapters 1 to 3 are drafted; the rest are plans. Each heading is tagged with a beat, so the Story Timeline (File → Story Timeline…) has a shape.

## Chapter 1: The Crossing (Inciting Incident)

Tam calls in the debt: take him to the drowned gate with the key that has already been through it.

## Chapter 2: Low Tide (Rising Action)

Ines Hale reads Mara's father out of the ledger. The key is spent, and Tam knew it.

## Chapter 3: The Gate Opens (Midpoint)

Mara cuts a new key from the old one. The gate opens, and a lamp is lit below.

## Chapter 4: Twenty Years of Oil (Rising Action)

The stairs, the lamp, and what has been kept burning down there, and by whom.

## Chapter 5: The Flood Bell (Climax)

The tide turns early. Mara must choose between going back and finishing what her father started.

## Chapter 6: Slack Water (Falling Action)

The reckoning with Tam: what the debt was always for.

## Chapter 7: Lamps (Resolution)

Northwatch at dusk. Someone else lights the lamps for once.
