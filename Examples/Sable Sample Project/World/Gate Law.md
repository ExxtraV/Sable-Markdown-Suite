# Gate Law

_Sample text written by Claude Code._

Every child on the coast learns it with their letters.

- **Every gate has a keeper.** Someone must always be there to open it and to write down who passed.
- **No key opens the same door twice.** A key is spent when it opens. The lock is not.
- **Gates open at low tide only.** Never before the bell, and never after.

Nobody has ever written down what the law says about a new key.
