# Chapter 2: Low Tide

_Sample text written by Claude Code._

The sea went out and left the flats shining. Tam grounded the boat and they walked the rest of the way, Mara's boots sucking at the mud.

The drowned gate stood a mile out, an arch of black iron up to its shoulders in silt. A woman in an oilskin coat was waiting beneath a lantern.

"Late," she said. "I'm Ines Hale. I keep this gate."

"Mara Venn."

"A key is spent when it opens," said Ines. "Your father's is finished."

"Then why am I here?"

Ines looked past her, at the ferryman.

"Ask him," she said.
