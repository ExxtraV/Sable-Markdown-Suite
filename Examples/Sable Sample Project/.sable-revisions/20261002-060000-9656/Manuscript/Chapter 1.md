# Chapter 1: The Crossing

_Sample text written by Claude Code._

Mara held the silver key against her palm. *Not yet*, she thought. Across the water, the lamps of Northwatch were going out.

The ferryman poled on in silence. She had meant to ask him about the debt, but it was the sort of thing that was easier not to say.

"The key won't work," she said at last. "It's been through the gate already."

"I know," said Tam.
