# Northwatch: Concept

_Sample text written by Claude Code._

## Logline

A locksmith with a debt to a ferryman must open a gate that, by law, cannot be opened with the only key she has.

## Writer's pitch

I wanted a fantasy where the magic is bookkeeping: tide tables, ledgers, and rules that have to be read very carefully. Mara solves problems with her hands, and this is a story where the answer is already written down and nobody has read it properly.

## Genre & tone

Quiet fantasy. Salt, rust, and lamplight; dry humor over real dread.

## Major themes

Debts, and who gets to name them. What we inherit from the people who went ahead of us. Rules as kindness, and rules as cages.

## Comparable titles

Small, atmospheric fantasies about work and weather, the kind that sit on the shelf between fairy tale and ghost story. This one is shorter on swords and longer on tide tables.

## Who is this for?

Readers who like their fantasy strange, specific, and a little damp.

## Central question

What do you owe the person who saved you, and what if they saved you for a reason?

## Notes

- Low tides are about twelve hours apart, so a chapter that spans two of them needs a reason.
- The lamp at the bottom of the stairs is not Mara's father. Decide who it is by chapter 5.
