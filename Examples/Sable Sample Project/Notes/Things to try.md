# Things to try

_Sample text written by Claude Code._

This is your own copy of the sample, so change anything you like. A few things worth a look:

- **Read a chapter.** Open Chapter 1. Names of characters, places, and world notes take a soft color as you read. Turn it off in View → Highlight Names & Places.
- **Click a chip.** The strip at the bottom of each chapter shows who and where. Click a chip to open its card without leaving the page. ⌃⌘T changes a scene's tags. Chapter 3 tags Mara by one of her aliases.
- **Open a card.** In the desk, hover Characters → Mara Venn and click the card icon. She has aliases and a portrait.
- **See the story's shape.** File → Story Timeline… (⌥⌘Y) plots the Outline's beats.
- **Compare drafts.** File → Revision History… (⌥⌘R) compares the saved snapshot with the chapters today. Chapter 3 shows as new.
- **Find the hidden note.** Chapter 2 has a note to self inside `<!-- -->`. It's dimmed on the page and never exported.
- **Go quiet.** ⇧⌘R for Reading Mode and ⇧⌘F for Paragraph Focus.

When you're done, move the folder to the Trash. Nothing else on your Mac is affected.
