# The Drowned Gate

_Sample text written by Claude Code._

## Description

An arch of black iron standing a mile out on the mud, buried to its shoulders in silt. It opens only at low tide, when the sea has gone far enough for the lock to be reached.

## History

Older than Northwatch, and no one remembers who built it. Its keeper's ledger goes back further than the city's records.

## Who is here

Ines Hale and her apprentice, Pell Arden, keep the gate.
