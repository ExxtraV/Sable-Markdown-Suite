# Northwatch

_Sample text written by Claude Code._

## Description

A harbor city of narrow stairs and slate roofs, built on the high ground above the old lower town. Its lamps go out street by street as the tide falls, because the lamplighters work to the tide tables and not the clock.

## History

The lower town flooded twenty years ago and was never rebuilt. The ferry stairs are all that is left of its waterfront.

## Who is here

Mara Venn keeps a locksmith's shop on Cooper's Stair. Tam Orrin ties up at the foot of it.
