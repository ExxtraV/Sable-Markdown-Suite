# Mara Venn

_Sample text written by Claude Code._

## Appearance

Narrow hands, ink and brass dust worked into the creases. A toolroll she never sets down.

## Personality

Careful, dry, and bad at asking for help. She counts things when she's afraid.

## Backstory

Pulled from the flooded lower town at nine. Her father, a locksmith before her, went through the drowned gate on the third night and did not come back.

## Relationships

Owes Tam Orrin a debt neither of them has ever added up.
