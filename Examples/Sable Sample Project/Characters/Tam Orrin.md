# Tam Orrin

_Sample text written by Claude Code._

## Appearance

Tall and stooped, gray as the water he works. He never looks back at Northwatch; he says it's bad for the steering.

## Personality

Patient to the point of cruelty. He never lies, but he answers only the question you ask.

## Relationships

He pulled Mara out of the flood and has been waiting twenty years to be paid.
