# Ines Hale

_Sample text written by Claude Code._

## Appearance

An oilskin coat, ink on her cuffs, a lantern always within reach.

## Personality

Exact. She believes a rule you can't bend is the kindest thing you can give a frightened person, and she is mostly right.

## Backstory

She has kept the gate for as long as the ledger has pages. Every key that has passed through is written in it.
