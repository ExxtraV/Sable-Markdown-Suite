# Sable's names and logo

_This policy is written by Claude Code._

Sable's **code** is free and open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE). You can use it, change it, and build on it.

Sable's **names and logo** are a different matter. They identify the official apps made by the Sable project maintainer (GitHub: [ExxtraV](https://github.com/ExxtraV)):

- **Sable Writer Suite**: the family of apps, at [sablewriter.app](https://sablewriter.app).
- **Sable Markdown Writer**: this app. "Sable" is its short name.
- **The Sable logo**: the sable curled nose to tail.

Other apps in the suite will be added here as they are released. The MIT License does not cover these names or the logo. This keeps people from being confused about which apps are the real ones, and that protects the writers who rely on them.

Other, unrelated products also use the word "Sable". This policy is only about the Sable Writer Suite's apps, their names, and the logo.

## Fine without asking

- Saying you use Sable, writing about it, reviewing it, or linking to it.
- Saying your project is "based on Sable" or "a fork of Sable" when it is.
- Sharing unmodified copies of an official release, under the Sable name and icon.
- Using the name and logo to point people to the official app or this repository, for example in an article or a list of writing tools.

## Please don't

- Release a modified version or fork of this app as "Sable", "Sable Markdown Writer", or as part of the "Sable Writer Suite", or with the Sable icon. Give it its own name and icon.
- Use a name or icon for a writing app that could be mistaken for one of the suite's, or suggest that your app, service, or product is official, endorsed, or part of the suite when it isn't.
- Sell Sable, or a lightly modified copy of it, under the Sable name.

## Questions

If you're not sure whether a use is fine, [open a discussion or issue](https://github.com/ExxtraV/Sable-Markdown-Suite/issues) and ask. Most reasonable requests will be a yes.

This policy isn't legal advice, and it doesn't limit anything the MIT License allows for the code itself.
